Privacy Policy - DM Helper

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Introduction

DM Helper ("we", "our", "the app") is a personal Instagram automation tool that helps Instagram creators automate direct message responses to comments and messages. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

2. Data We Collect

When you connect your Instagram account, we collect:

  • Your Instagram username, account ID, and profile picture
  • An access token to interact with the Instagram API on your behalf
  • Comments and messages needed to run the automations you configure

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used only to provide the automation features you set up (for example: auto-replying to comments containing a keyword you choose). We do not use your data for any other purpose.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a private database (Supabase) controlled by the app owner. Access tokens are stored securely and never displayed publicly.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party, except as required to interact with Meta's Instagram API to deliver the features you requested.

6. Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time by disconnecting your Instagram account from the app and emailing us at yashps2008@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". We will remove your tokens and account data within 7 days.

7. Contact

For any privacy questions, contact: yashps2008@gmail.com
